# Features of ChatGPT
One of the Chatbots available to BYU-Idaho students is ChatGPT, a multimodal GenAI tool developed by OpenAI. It functions as a family of AI models built to process and generate text, code, images, audio, and video that can access custom GPTs and Plugins.

Beyond the basics of using AI Tools, found here: [How To Use AI Tools](how-to-use-ai-tools.md), this guide helps Copywriters understand and use the features of ChatGPT.

**How ChatGPT Operates**
* The website and app **[https://chatgpt.com/](https://chatgpt.com/)** functions as an multimodal GenAI chatbot.
* Beyond the stand-alone chat window, ChatGPT supports voice on web, iOS, and Android; it can also work with browsers and computers through agent tools.
* ChatGPT provides the AI features in developer platforms like **[Open AI Developers]([https://aistudio.google.com/prompts/new_chat?project=gen-lang-client-0122606831](https://developers.openai.com/))**.

**Why Use ChatGPT?**

* **Custom GPTs:** Copywriters can use or create custom GPTs that give AI a specific role or function. These can also be given content that it will always reference and access when generating responses. 
* **AI Agents:** Operates as a independent and automatic assist that can complete specific tasks in various software applications such as Outlook, Teams, or even Google Workspace  
* **Plugins:** Allows specific tasks to be connected with specific apps, integrating external applications into chats and projects.
* **Models:** Allows precise control over a variety of models with different capabilities by giving the user control over the rate of tokenization.

##### *Note On Data Privacy*

> Official *@byui.edu* accounts are protected by enterprise-grade data boundaries. Unlike free personal accounts where prompts can be used to train public models, any submissions on the university network are completely private.
> 
> They are never used to train Open AI's models, never reviewed by humans, and remain strictly within university control. However, data such as SSNs, bank account information, or anything else should never be shared with these tools.

## Getting Started With ChatGPT

### Signing Up

Here is the step-by-step instructions to setting up ChatGPT:

> #### 1. **Go to ChatGPT**.
> * Navigate to **[https://chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)**. Click the **"Log In"** button.  
> * *(If you are already signed in to a personal account, click your profile icon and select Log out or switch accounts).*


> #### 2. **Enter Your BYU-Idaho Email Address**.
> * On the sign-in page, enter your full **BYU-Idaho email address** (e.g., *username@byui.edu*) and click Continue.
> * *(You must use your full **@byui.edu email** address rather than just your username).*


> #### 3. **Complete Authentication**.
> * You will be redirected to the single sign-on page. Enter your Church account credentials and complete **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** if prompted.
> * Once authenticated, you will return to ChatGPT. Click your profile icon to ensure your *@byui.edu* email is listed.
> * *(You may be signed in automatically if you already have an active BYU-Idaho SSO session in your browser).*

> [!WARNING]
>:warning: **Use Your Institutional Account**
> 
> ***If you use a personal account, no institutional data protections are in place—treat it as public-level only. Remember it will train on your conversations.***

### Library 
ChatGPT has a *Library* feature that stores images and documents for easy access found in the sidebar. Copywriters can reference both uploaded and generated materials here.

### Scheduled Tasks
ChatGPT has a *Scheduled Tasks* features that allows users to set reminders found in the sidebar. Notifications must be allowed for ChatGPT to notify on devices when it is not actively opened.
