#### Using AI as a Source
AI tools should not be cited or used as a primary or secondary source. 



Plagiarism includes:  

Citing a source that does not exist. 

Creating fake information, graphs, or words and stating it from an official author or source. 

Claiming that results or information comes from an author or source when the original author or source did not claim those results. 

Citing an author or source in a reference section or bibliography or works cited section when the author or source is not referenced in the assignment. 

Purposefully changing the meaning of words, or information from another source or author.  

Creating fake data or results to support conclusions.
