# AI Copywriting Manual

## Instructions

What we will need to cover are:

> * What is Artificial Intelligence?
> * How to Leverage AI for Research
> * How to Use AI to Create Content Outlines
> * How to Get Ideas from AI without the Output Sounding Generic, Formulaic, or Robotic
> * How to Write Effective Prompts that Captures Specific Brand, Tone, Voice, and Audience Pain Points
> * AI and Ethics (copyright, plagiarism, etc.)
> * How to Make Your Writing Different from "AI Slop" (writers should focus on what AI cannot do, such as human storytelling, personal experience, and real-time cultural context, etc.)
> * Checking Sources and AI Hallucinations
> * Anything else you feel is applicable
